
LOGISTICS BUSINESS CONTEXT

Driver Performance:
- On-time delivery rate measures the percentage
  of deliveries completed on schedule.
- Fuel efficiency is measured in MPG.
- Revenue per mile measures generated revenue
  divided by driven miles.

Fleet Utilization:
- Truck utilization measures how effectively
  fleet assets are used.
- Revenue per truck measures revenue generated
  by each truck.

Route Profitability:
- Route profitability compares revenue against
  associated operating costs.

Customer Performance:
- Customer revenue measures total shipment revenue.
- Service level measures delivery performance.
